# Brag Plan: BetterCricket Selection Whiteboard

## What is this app?
The Selection Whiteboard is the BetterSelect screen that puts every team playing on a matchday side by side, with the pool of players nobody has picked yet. Availability is colour coded on every card, and selectors drag players between teams, then confirm.

## The angle
A BetterCricket advert about how the whiteboard streamlines selection meetings for clubs. It ties back to availability: players answer on the club's availability link (or their Player Dashboard) and the answer shows up on the whiteboard straight away. Same simple style as the Player Dashboard video (BetterCricket green on dark, slow dissolves, music only), with a camera that zooms into parts of the large screen.

## Beats (on-screen text, about 113s)
1. Intro, 0-7s: "Introducing BetterSelect Selection Whiteboard" with a stylised whiteboard drawing (a player is picked up from the pool and dropped into the 3rd XI)
2. Availability through the link, 7-21s: the link is pasted into a WhatsApp group, players pick their name and PIN, one tap on the Player Dashboard page
3. Answers land on the whiteboard, 21-37s: the camera zooms to Jack Barendse's card (no answer yet, grey edge), his answer arrives and the edge turns green; then "Hide unavailable" in one click
4. Every team, side by side, 37-48s: five teams and the pool on one screen, camera pans across the team headers and fill counts
5. Availability, colour coded, 48-65.5s: Available, Maybe, Unavailable, No response, then a W1 player and a W2 player side by side with their weekend ticks and colours, then the 7 Nov tab showing a two-day game on both weekends
6. Drag and drop, 65.5-82.4s: pool to team, a swap between two teams where both sides change at once, filling sides, autosaved drafts
7. Confirm, 82.4-90s: Confirm all (4), then All confirmed
8. Audit History, 90-99s: who changed what, and when, including a player's own answer from their link
9. Collaboration, 99-107.5s: "Selection, together." One shared board, the same live availability for everyone, drag and drop with no training
10. End card, 107.5-112.9s

## Tone
- Preset: app-store (clean, benefit-led), same as the Player Dashboard video
- Background: soft drifting aurora in BetterSelect blue (with cyan and violet), accents in BetterSelect blue, BetterCricket logo bug top right
- Interpretation: one idea per scene, one caption at a time, camera zooms instead of busy overlays

## Format: landscape 1920x1080, about 113s

## Data
The screens are the real Selection Whiteboard running locally with a stubbed API. Applecross Cricket Club branding. All player names, availability answers, lineups and fixtures are demo data (fictional names, except Jack Barendse as the demo player). The audit rows are demo data too. Fixtures are Saturday 31 Oct 2026, with a two-day 1st XI game running to 7 Nov.

## Audio
- No voiceover. Music: bundled happy-beats vol 1, steady under, fade out over the last 3s
- SFX: taps, soft card slides on scene changes, card placements on each drop, a bell on the end card
