# Brag Plan: BetterCricket Selection Whiteboard

## What is this app?
The Selection Whiteboard is the BetterSelect screen that puts every team playing on a matchday side by side, with the pool of players nobody has picked yet. Availability is colour coded on every card, and selectors drag players between teams, then confirm.

## The angle
A BetterCricket advert about how the whiteboard streamlines selection meetings for clubs. It ties back to availability: players answer on the club's availability link (or their Player Dashboard) and the answer shows up on the whiteboard straight away. Same simple style as the Player Dashboard video (BetterCricket green on dark, slow dissolves, music only), with a camera that zooms into parts of the large screen.

## Beats (on-screen text, about 101s)
1. Intro, 0-6s: "Introducing BetterCricket Selection Whiteboard"
2. Availability through the link, 6-21s: link in the club chat, pick your name and PIN, one tap on the Player Dashboard page
3. Answers land on the whiteboard, 21-35s: Jack's answer appears, card colours update, "Hide unavailable" in one click
4. Every team, side by side, 35-46s: five teams and the pool on one screen, camera pans across the team headers and fill counts
5. W1 and W2, 46-63s: the legend, "W1 ONLY", "W2 ?", "W2 ONLY", the half-available colours, and a two-day game on both weekend tabs
6. Drag and drop, 63-79.9s: pool to team, a swap between two teams where both sides change at once, filling sides, autosaved drafts
7. Confirm, 79.9-87.5s: Confirm all (4), then All confirmed
8. Benefits, 87.5-96s: "Less chasing. Shorter meetings."
9. End card, 96-101.2s

## Tone
- Preset: app-store (clean, benefit-led), same as the Player Dashboard video
- Interpretation: one idea per scene, one caption at a time, camera zooms instead of busy overlays

## Format: landscape 1920x1080, about 101s

## Data
The screens are the real Selection Whiteboard running locally with a stubbed API. Applecross Cricket Club branding. All player names, availability answers, lineups and fixtures are demo data (fictional names, except Jack Barendse as the demo player). Fixtures are Saturday 31 Oct 2026, with a two-day 1st XI game running to 7 Nov.

## Audio
- No voiceover. Music: bundled happy-beats vol 1, steady under, fade out over the last 3s
- SFX: taps, soft card slides on scene changes, card placements on each drop, a bell on the end card
