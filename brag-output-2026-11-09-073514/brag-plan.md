# Brag Plan: BetterSocials (BetterPosts)

## What is this app?
BetterSocials is the BetterCricket module for a club's social media. BetterPosts is its post editor: pick a type of post and one click pulls in the latest lineups, fixtures, results, milestones and more, already laid out in the club's colours and ready to share.

## The angle
A BetterCricket advert about volunteer time. Posts that used to take an evening in a design tool now take one click, and every colour can still be changed. New templates keep arriving. Same simple style as the other videos: module-colour aurora (BetterSocials pink with violet and rose), music only, real screens, camera zooms.

## Beats (on-screen text, about 118s)
1. Intro, 0-7s: "Introducing BetterSocials. Posts in one click." with a fan of real posts
2. Pick a post, 7-17s: the real BetterPosts start screen, "Every layout arrives pre-filled with your club's data"
3. One click, 17-56s: six real pulls, each with the cursor clicking once and the post filling in: fixtures, results, lineup (from BetterSelect), player of the match (paste a match link), milestones, team of the week
4. Hours back for volunteers, 56-69s: the by-hand chores (copy the lineup, type the scores, find the photos, match the colours, line it up, export) against one click
5. Every colour, your way, 69-91s: the real Brand panel: palettes, backgrounds, colour roles, fonts, all changing the post live
6. New layouts, all the time, 91-103s: a wall of real layouts with NEW tags ("70+ layouts" is the count on the live start screen)
7. Closing, 103-112s: "Great posts, in minutes."
8. End card, 112-118s

## Tone
- Preset: app-store (clean, benefit-led), same as the other videos
- Background: drifting aurora in BetterSocials pink, violet and rose; BetterCricket logo bug top right
- Interpretation: one idea per scene, one caption at a time, zooms to the button being clicked and then to the finished post

## Format: landscape 1920x1080, about 118s

## Data
The screens are the real BetterPosts editor running locally with a stubbed API. Applecross Cricket Club branding and crest. Player names and photos are real Applecross players (original hi-res photos from the Player Cards work; no Jack Barendse in this one). Career figures behind the milestones (for example Matt Campbell's 400+ games, Ramon Fletcher's 700+ wickets, Len Blakey's 5,000+ runs) come from those real career numbers. The fixtures, results, scores and milestone dates are demo data.

## Audio
- No voiceover. Music: bundled happy-beats vol 1, steady under, fade out over the last 3s
- SFX: clicks on each pull, soft card slides on scene changes, a pop when a post lands, a bell on the end card
