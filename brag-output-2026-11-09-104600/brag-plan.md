# Brag Plan: BetterSocials, template by template

## Angle
One-click posts for volunteers, shown template by template on the real BetterPosts editor. Each template uses a different real club's crest, sponsors and adult player photos; opposition crests pull through automatically. Same style as the other videos: BetterSocials pink aurora, music only, camera zooms, BetterCricket logo bug.

## Scenes (about 4.5 minutes)
Intro, start screen, T11 (Applecross: transparent cut-out photo, milestone, debut, captain, sponsor), T12 (Hilton Bicton: sponsors from Stats data), FX1 (Scarborough: crests and adding a page), FX3 (Kalamunda), C4 (Applecross), RS7 (Hilton Bicton: background photo), RR7 (Applecross), RR2 (Bannockburn), MS1 (Hilton Bicton), MS2 (Scarborough), TW1 (Kalamunda), TW2 (High Wycombe), Events (Gisborne: layouts, examples, calendar, adding an event), C5 (Applecross announcement), drag and drop customisation (Gisborne), template wall, close, end card.

## Data
Real club crests, sponsors and player photos from public BetterCricket pages. Adult players only, placeholder shirt photos excluded, no Jack Barendse. Milestone and debut flags use real career totals; fixtures, scores and match performances are demo data. Opposition clubs shown are real clubs but the fixtures are made up.
