NEW SINCE YOU LAST LOOKED

Junior stats, kept separate
  Junior grades are picked out for you and can be left out of senior records,
  so an Under 14s season stops sitting inside a first grade average. Your club
  sets which grades count.
  https://betterat.cricket/modules/betterstats

Vote counting that runs itself
  Players vote from their phone after the game and the count keeps itself up to
  date. Run 3-2-1 or set your own values, decide who votes and how ties are
  broken, then read the season out on presentation night.
  https://betterat.cricket/modules/betterselect

BetterSocials, rebuilt
  It now works like Canva, with your scores, players, photos and club colours
  already in the template. Drag things where you want them and the post is done
  before you have left the ground.
  https://betterat.cricket/modules/bettersocials
