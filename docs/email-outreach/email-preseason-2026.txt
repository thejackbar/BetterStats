Hi [first name],

Every game your club has ever played is already sitting with Cricket Australia,
as far back as the records go. We pull the lot and turn it into a live site: a
profile for every player who has turned out for the club, leaderboards,
all-time records, every scorecard and a yearbook for each season.

We find the errors in it too. Most clubs have the same player recorded three
different ways, hundreds filed against the wrong season, and career totals that
quietly stopped adding up years ago. Those get merged, reconciled and fixed, and
if your older records only live in a spreadsheet we bring those in as well.

After that you never import anything again. Every scorecard flows in on its own
after the game. We set it up with you, run a demo for your committee, and stay
on hand through your first season.
