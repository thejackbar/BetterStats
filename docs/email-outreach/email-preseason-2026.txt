DON'T CUT CORNERS WITH YOUR STATS

A spreadsheet kept by four different people over ten years is full of errors,
and everyone knows it. The same bloke entered three different ways. A hundred
filed against the wrong season. Career totals that quietly stopped adding up
in 2019.

We pull your history straight from Cricket Australia, then we go looking for
those errors. Duplicate players get merged, seasons that were recorded twice
get reconciled, and the figures that never added up get fixed. If your old
records only live in a spreadsheet, we bring those in and clean them up too.

Most clubs end up with records more accurate than they have ever had.
