Quick question: how many hours did your volunteers spend this week sorting availability, picking teams, posting to socials and chasing up fees?
If you're like most clubs we know, the answer is "too many." We know because we've been those volunteers. We're Jack and Elton, and between us we've done just about every thankless job in community cricket.
We waited for PlayHQ to build what clubs actually asked for. Club history. Easy availability and selection. Help with the social media grind. It never came. So we built BetterCricket ourselves.
It handles availability, selection, socials, fees and admin. On top of that, it gives your club statistical analysis nobody's ever put in front of community cricketers before.
