New since you last looked
Junior stats, kept separate  NEW
Junior grades are picked out for you and can be left out of senior records, so an Under 14s season stops sitting inside a first grade average. Your club sets which grades count.

Vote counting that runs itself  NEW
Players vote from their phone after the game and the count keeps itself up to date. Run 3-2-1 or set your own values, decide who votes and how ties are broken, then read the season out on presentation night.
