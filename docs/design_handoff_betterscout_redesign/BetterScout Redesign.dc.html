Turn 1 · Redesign onto house module chrome
BetterScout, wearing the module shell
Press Box tokens, the 232px ModuleLayout sidebar every other module uses, and a scout brand of magenta-violet #C026D3 — violet family like BetterIQ, its analytical cousin, without wearing IQ's exact accent. Player photos have a reserved slot everywhere a player appears, falling back to the initials avatar the club-side comparison page already ships. Marks in the module switcher are the real assets/modules SVGs; BetterScout has no mark upstream yet, so its lockup uses an accent tile as a placeholder. Sample data: Applecross Cricket Club, tracked by a placement agency.
