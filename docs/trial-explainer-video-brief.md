# BetterStats explainer video: production brief

Status: **draft for sign-off.** Nothing is rendered yet. Rendering costs credits and takes minutes, so the script is agreed first.

## What it is for

BetterStats is the core of BetterCricket. This video shows what a club gets and how little work it takes: go to /trial, sync, bring in the old history, merge the duplicates, and the club's site fills in. It ends on the other modules and sends people back to /trial.

- **Master:** about 1:55, for /trial, /videos and organic.
- **Condensed cut:** about 45 seconds, built from the master's strongest scenes, to test as the Meta ad.
- **Hook and call to action:** the sign-up. The middle is the product.

## Specs

- **Format:** 4:5, 1080 x 1350, 30 fps. Everything important sits inside the centre 1080 x 1080 so a 1:1 export crops cleanly.
- **Sound:** captions only, no voiceover. Many people watch with sound off. A quiet instrumental bed is optional (see open items).
- **Look:** simplified UI on the product's dark navy, brand green for BetterStats, each module in its own colour at the end. Not the exact UI. Each screen needs real motion: things arrive, connect, fill and count, they don't just cut.
- **Fonts and colours:** from the brand theme, so the video matches the site.
- **Names:** fictional players only, and no junior names anywhere. Club crests are real, with permission.

## Pacing rules (the "don't rush" rules)

1. A point's motion finishes, then the screen **holds for at least 2 seconds** before the next thing starts.
2. A caption appears half a second after its motion starts and stays until a second after the motion ends.
3. A caption is 12 words or fewer and stays up long enough to read at 3 words a second, so 10 words needs at least 3.5 seconds.
4. One idea per screen. If a screen needs two captions, they follow each other, they don't stack.
5. Nothing important sits in the top 120 px or bottom 200 px (Feed UI covers them).

## Script

Times are targets. Each scene's last second is a hold.

### 1. Hook (0:00 to 0:09)
- **Motion:** a search box on dark navy. A club name types in, results slide up as crest cards, the box clears and a different club types in. All seven crests appear in turn.
- **Captions:** "Your club's whole cricket history. Kept for you." (0:01) then "Start at betterat.cricket/trial" (0:06).

### 2. Sign up (0:09 to 0:21), Gisborne
- **Motion:** the Gisborne crest card enlarges. A progress bar runs across three simple steps: details, a 4-digit email code (four boxes fill one by one), terms ticked. A clock badge reads "about 3 minutes".
- **Captions:** "Pick your club." (0:10) then "A few details and a 4-digit email code." (0:14) then "Free trial. No card." (0:18).

### 3. First sync, then Sync Now (0:21 to 0:35), Applecross
- **Motion:** the dashboard appears and a ring starts filling on its own. Counters roll up for games, players and seasons (labelled "Example club"). Then the Data Sync screen: the **Sync Now** button is pressed, a new game card slides in and a leaderboard row moves up one place.
- **Captions:** "Your first sync starts on its own." (0:22) then "It pulls your club's history from Cricket Australia." (0:26) then "Later, one click on Sync Now brings in the latest games." (0:30).

### 4. Bring in your history (0:35 to 0:57)
Four short beats, about 5 seconds each, with a one-second hold at the end.

- **4a. Spreadsheet (Sunbury):** a big spreadsheet drops in. Header cells connect by animated lines to the fields they match (Player, Season, Runs). **Caption:** "Drop in any spreadsheet. Match the columns once."
- **4b. Scorecard photo (Bannockburn):** a photo of a handwritten scorebook page. A scan line sweeps down it and the numbers fill a tidy scorecard, then a review tick. **Caption:** "Photograph an old scorecard. It reads it, you check it."
- **4c. Partnership records:** a file lands and partnership bars grow into a table. **Caption:** "Add partnership records from a file."
- **4d. Awards:** pick an award type, a player and a season, and an honour-board line appears. **Caption:** "Add club awards and honours."

### 5. Merge duplicates (0:57 to 1:07), Leeming Spartans
- **Motion:** two player cards with different spellings of one name slide together and fuse. A counter reads "12 pairs found", a button is pressed and it drops to 0 for the exact matches. A smaller group stays, marked "check these".
- **Captions:** "Same player, spelled two ways? We find the pairs." (0:58) then "Merge the exact matches in one click." (1:02) then "Check the rest pair by pair." (1:05).

### 6. Your site fills in (1:07 to 1:25)
- **6a. Leaderboards (Applecross):** bars race into rank order, then filter chips switch season and grade. **Caption:** "Leaderboards for every category."
- **6b. Records (Kalamunda):** a records board lists bests, a new record drops in with a flag. **Caption:** "All-time records, flagged the week they're set."
- **6c. Player profile (Scarborough):** a career page builds: totals count up, a season curve draws, milestone badges pop. **Caption:** "A career page for every player."
- First caption: "Now it all fills in." (1:07). Last second: the same pages shown together on a laptop and a phone.

### 7. StatLab (1:25 to 1:35), Applecross
- **Motion:** a long list of report groups opens, one report is picked and a table builds. Then filter chips toggle and the table reshapes.
- **Captions:** "StatLab: 200+ ready-made reports." (1:26) then "Or build your own with filters." (1:30).

### 8. And more (1:35 to 1:43)
Four tiles, about 2 seconds each. Each is a short caption only, not an explanation.

- "Every scorecard, kept for good." / "A season annual that fills itself." / "Honour boards and premierships." / "A card for every player."

### 9. The other modules (1:43 to 1:55)
- **Motion:** the BetterStats tile (green) sits in the centre. The others arrive one at a time with their logo and colour, each held about 2.5 seconds.
- **Captions:** "Once Stats is loaded, add what you need." (1:43) then BetterSelect, blue: "Availability and team selection." then BetterSocials, pink: "Posts that fill from your match data." then BetterAdmin, amber: "Fees, email, rosters and meetings." then BetterIQ, purple: "Know the opposition." and BetterFantasy, cyan: "A club fantasy comp."

### 10. Call to action (1:55 to 2:01)
- **Motion:** the search box returns and types a club name. The BetterCricket logo settles above it.
- **Captions:** "Type your club's name." then "Free trial. No card." then "betterat.cricket/trial". Final frame holds 3 seconds.

## How it gets built

1. Hyperframes composition (HTML, CSS and JS), one scene per section so a scene can be changed without redoing the rest. Deterministic motion, no filmed footage.
2. Inputs: the seven crests, the module logos and colours from the brand registry, and the existing product screenshots as visual references.
3. Render the 4:5 master and a 1:1 centre crop, then cut the 45-second version from the same scenes.
4. Review each scene on a phone with the sound off before moving on.

## Crests

Seen so far (Bannockburn, Gisborne, Sunbury, sent as images, not yet saved as files):
