# Guide: Player Cards (collectable cards, packs, the card book)

**Read this before**:
- Touching `services/player_cards.py`, `player_cards_ddl.py`, `routers/public_player_cards.py`, `routers/player_cards_admin.py`, `components/cards/*`, `CardsModule.jsx`, `AdminPlayerCards.jsx`.
- Changing what the player page (`/playerdash/:token`) needs from BetterSelect, or `player_dashboard.py` module defaults.
- Adding a table that records what a person owns or earned (it goes on `merge_carry.CARRIED`).

**Archive** (full history, do not load whole): `docs/dev-notes/archive/player-cards.md`.

**Related guides**: `betterselect-selection-and-nets` (the player page and its PIN), `cross-club-and-data-safety` (`merge_carry`), `stats-figures-records-and-filters` (the figures cards read), `sponsors-tiers-and-naming` (the sponsor chip on a card, and who picks it).

## Standing rules

1. **A regular card is derived, never stored.** Figures and the 1 to 99 rarity come from `club_snapshot` (cached 5 minutes per club; `forget(org)` after a settings or hide change). What a person owns is `player_card_copies`, one row per copy, so a double is a second row. A player's own card is implied and is never a row.
2. **Grants and cards only ever ADD.** A pack is unique on (player, source, ref_key); a figure corrected downwards never takes one back. A special card is unique on (club, event_key) and a score corrected later keeps its card (it commemorates the moment). Hiding a player removes their card from packs and galleries; it deletes nothing, and showing them again brings it back. Never delete a pack, copy or special card.
3. **Every player-keyed table here is on `merge_carry.CARRIED`** (owner and subject columns both), so a merge moves a collection. A new such table needs the same entry plus `_OWN_CLUB_TABLES`.
4. **The pool**: club players with `is_player`, minus `is_public` false (this is also how a removal request is honoured), minus `player_card_hidden`, minus junior-only players unless `include_juniors`, and only players with a match are drawable. Never widen it for a convenience.
5. **Packs**: 2 starter, the club's weekly count per Monday-to-Sunday week in the club's timezone (default `Australia/Perth`; only the weekly pack expires), 1 per 25 runs / 2 wickets / 2 dismissals in the club's CURRENT season (newest played game, as year and kind), 1 per duck since `club_card_settings.launched_at`. Never `ORDER BY year DESC, name DESC` for the season.
6. **Odds**: Common 60, Rare 28, Epic 10, Legendary 2; a special card 5% of the time when the club has any; one Rare or better guaranteed; no repeat in a pack. `draw_cards` is pure so the odds are testable. There are no paid packs. Do not add any.
7. **Special cards** are made by `refresh_specials` (throttled 10 minutes per club, `force` from the admin button), each with a stable `event_key` (player, date, innings) so pairing a manual game with a synced one does not make a second card. Add a kind in `SPECIAL_KINDS`, the insert in `refresh_specials`, `cardTheme.SPECIALS` and `specialHeadline`. An award card reads the club's display name for the award (`org_award_definitions.display_name`, one definition picked by `sort_order`) and the season the way the honour board does (a seasons id becomes its name, `2015_16` reads `2015/16`), and `refresh_specials` brings an existing award or hat-trick card in line (title and detail only). Career milestones (`STAT_MILESTONES`: every 1,000 runs, 200 wickets, 50 dismissals, capped 30/10/20 a player) key on `kind:player:threshold`, are dated only when the held scorecards can place the step, and file with the games milestones in the book.
8. **The player page no longer needs BetterSelect.** `_club_for_token(need_select=False)` serves the landing, PIN, `/me`, profile, ranks and details; the availability write (`set_me`) still needs it. A club without it gets `has_availability: false` and no Availability section. Keep `SELECT_ONLY` modules blocked for it.
9. **A PATCH reads `model_fields_set`**: an absent key leaves the setting alone, `timezone: null` clears it.
10. **Swaps** (`services/player_card_swaps.py`): any card a player HOLDS can go, a single copy as well as a double (`tradeable()`, one row = one copy). Their own card is implied and has no row, so it never goes; only copies of it they have pulled do. A request is MANY cards each way (`player_card_swap_items`: side, card, qty; up to 50 different cards a side, 99 copies of one), nothing moves until the person asked accepts, and the accept re-checks every card under row locks (owner id order, then card order) and moves all of it or none of it; a stale request answers 409. The old `give_*` / `want_*` columns on `player_card_swaps` are only read by migration 366's backfill; new requests leave them empty. A gifted special that is swapped away takes the subject's own commemorative copy with it (their choice). A request is never deleted. Only the cards another player holds are readable by a player. No separate sign in: the PIN cookie is the identity and the second person's yes is the safeguard. A swapped copy is marked `source = 'swap'`. The route still reads the first release's `give_key` / `want_key`. Someone else's cards are browsed in the swap builder with `CardGallery` in `browse` mode (same book, sort, filters, stacks, plus a search box and a quick pick button on every card; no grey slots, no Newest sort, the badge is how many could go), for theirs and for yours. `GET /me/cards/swaps/find?q=` (`find_cards`) searches the whole club by player name, special title or kind, season or grade and returns each card with the players holding a copy that could go (never the asker, never anyone out of the pool, never how many beyond what could go), and the Ask button opens the builder with that person and card chosen. Anything that reads another player's cards must go through `tradeable()`. The Club cards tab (`CatalogueTab`, `services/player_card_catalogue.py`, `GET /me/cards/catalogue` and `/catalogue/holders`) shows the club's whole set, held or not, as the same book: the server lays out one window at a time (a club can have tens of thousands of special cards; only the window's cards are built), and `CardGallery` runs in `remote` mode. A card carries `others` (players who could swap a copy) and `mine_copies`, never anyone's holdings beyond that; keep `CATEGORIES` in the service and in `CardGallery.jsx` in step.
11. **The book** files specials awards, milestones, centuries, half centuries, wickets, debuts (`CATEGORIES` in `CardGallery.jsx`; add a kind to one of them) and stacks one player's cards of one kind on one slot. Grey slots are for player cards only.
12. **Text on a card goes through `FitText`**, never a length based font size: it measures, so a long name, a long award or a wide club font cannot clip. Italic type needs room on the right (`FitText` adds it); never `overflow: hidden` on display type. Cards use the club's display font through `useCardStyle`.
13. **Sharing**: a picture for any card, and for the player's own card a link and a GIF made on the device (`lib/cardGif.js`). Rendering goes through `renderNodeBlob`, so the club's font is embedded as on the share card.
14. **The admin view** (`services/player_card_admin.py`) only reads. A card is drawn only for a player in the pool; anyone else appears as a line of text. "Expired" is derived from age and never written.
15. **Frontend**: card faces are drawn flat at `CARD_W x CARD_H` and scaled with `ScaledCard`. The gallery measures its own width (phone 2x2, tablet 3x2, spread two pages), so the player page's cards section spans every column (`Group wide`). Every animation is off under reduced motion. `innerText` of a card returns capitals (CSS uppercase), write checks that way.

16. **The page** (v9.132.0): Player Cards is `pages/playercards/PlayerCardsApp.jsx` at `/playerdash/:token/cards/*`, rendered by `PublicAvailability` (which does the PIN check) when the path under the token starts with `cards`. Screens: Home, `book`, `club`, `packs`, `swaps` (+ `sent`, `history`, `new`), `how`; shared state is `context.jsx` (cards, packs, swap count, the pack opening overlays live in the shell). Navigation is `nav.jsx`: sidebar at lg, rail at md, bottom bar of five below that, drawn on a dark ground (`data-theme="dark"`) whatever the site theme. A new screen is a route in `PlayerCardsApp`, an entry in `nav.NAV`, and a `data-screen` on its root (the browser suites wait on it). Links are always absolute (`basePath`). The dashboard section is only `CardsSummary`. The dashboard itself moved to `/playerdash/:token`; `/avail/:token/*` is `LegacyAvailRedirect` and must keep working (links are pinned in group chats and on posters); every link the app creates uses `/playerdash`. `playerdash` is on the four reserved-root lists, `clubPath`, and nginx's manifest map. The football silo keeps its own `/avail` route.

17. **The social side** (v9.132.1, migration 368, `services/player_card_social_ddl.py`): `player_card_wants` (private wish list; capped at 100; never your own card or one you hold), `player_card_open_offers` ("open to offers" on a spare: the holder sees how many players want it, a number and never names), `player_card_prefs` (alerts, defaults on, a missing row is the defaults; a PUT reads `model_fields_set`), `player_card_alerts` (the log; the partial unique index on live `sent` and `skipped` rows is what stops a second wish list email until `scan_wants` clears it because nobody could swap the card any more), `player_card_swaps.parent_swap_id` and `.note` (a counter offer is a NEW request from the person asked, and the first ends `countered`; a note is cut to 200 characters). All five tables are on `merge_carry.CARRIED` (prefs keyed by `player_id`, hence `_ROW_KEY`). Alert email (`services/player_card_alerts.py`) goes after the request committed, on its own session, through `selection_notify._deliver` as operational mail: no live provider is a `failed` log row and never a send, under 18s and addresses that are not deliverable are skipped, the club can switch it all off (`club_card_settings.alerts_enabled`) and a player can switch each kind. Never email on a path that has not been gated by `_gate`. Spares are copies beyond the one a player keeps (every pulled copy of their own card). The card page is `/cards/card/:key`; keys in a path are `encodeURIComponent`'d.

18. **The community side** (v9.132.2, migration 369, `services/player_card_community_ddl.py`): sets are defined in code and derived on read (`services/player_card_sets.py`: a set needs 3 cards; tiers, special kinds, a season's awards; finishing one earns a banner and nothing else, never extra packs). The catalogue reads a set through `set_key` (so a set page is the club book filtered). The showcase (`player_card_showcase`, up to 6, order in `slot`, not unique so a merge cannot collide) only ever shows cards the owner holds, checked on save and on read. The collectors board is OPT OUT since v9.132.3 (everyone with a card is on it; `player_card_prefs.board_hidden` leaves it; `board_opt_in` is no longer read), ranks by distinct cards over all cards (never copies) and shows name, showcase and rarest player card only. The feed (`feed_hidden` opt out) is built from dated special cards, big pulls and accepted swaps of the last 7 days, and leaves out anyone who opted out. The club switches (`sets_enabled`, `showcase_enabled`, `board_enabled`, `feed_enabled`, plus `alerts_enabled`) reach the page as `features` on `/me/cards`; a switched-off route answers 404, the nav hides the entry (`nav.NAV[].feature`) and `Sets` redirects to the first part that is on. The admin Activity tab has Most wanted (counts of wish list demand, never whose list).
19. **More packs, minimal gifts, quiet defaults** (v9.132.3, migration 370, `services/player_card_packs_ddl.py`, `services/player_card_pack_kinds.py`): every pack source is a `Kind` (odds, how many of five are Rare or better, special chance); `draw_cards(pool, specials, size, kind)` takes it and `open_pack` picks it from the pack's source. Standard sources are unchanged (60/28/10/2, one Rare+, 5%). Day packs (`match_day`, holidays, `birthday`, `mystery`, `event`) are 2 Rare+; achievement packs (`century` 4, `five_for` and `hat_trick` 4, `fifty` 3, `milestone` 3) are stacked. Day packs are granted only when the player opens their page that day (club-local date, `club_today`), then bank; a missed day is missed. Their unique key is `(player, source, ref)` with the date or year in `ref`. Surprise days are `sha256(org, date) mod 12`, nothing stored. Achievement packs come from `player_special_cards.occurred_on >= launched_at` and never backfill. `club_card_settings.event_packs_enabled` switches the calendar, birthday, surprise and Match Day packs; the club's scheduled packs (`club_card_event_packs`, at most 31 days a run, 60 in all, label stored on the pack row) are not affected by it. Starting gifts: `starter_gifts` is `minimal` (debut + the latest landmark card; kinds in `LANDMARK_KINDS`) or `all`; gifts stay add-only, and `trim_gifts` (dry run unless `apply`, audited) deletes only copies still marked `gift` and held by their subject, so a swap (which re-marks the copy) is never undone. Prefs now default OFF for both alert emails (`load_prefs` and the column default); the board is on for all and `board_hidden` opts out. Pack art is drawn once at 220x330 and scaled with a transform, so never size its type from the width.
20. **Pack designs** (v9.132.5, migration 371, `services/player_card_designs.py`, `player_card_designs_ddl.py`): a club's own look for its packs, one row per TARGET in `club_card_pack_designs` (`default`, a pack source, or `event:<id>`). A pack takes its event's design, then its source's, then `default`, then the built-in look (`resolve`); `available_packs` attaches `design` to each pack and `/me/cards` sends the club's whole map as `pack_designs` for the "how packs arrive" screen. The `default` design's `title` is never used as a pack name; an event pack keeps the event's own name. Images (`bg`, `art`) are decoded, shrunk (1200 and 900) and RE-ENCODED with Pillow before they are stored, which bounds the size and strips EXIF; SVG is refused because it is served from our own origin. They live in Postgres and are served at `/api/images/card-packs/{id}/{bg|art}?v=<ms>` (the `?v=` is `bg_rev`/`art_rev`, so the bytes at a URL never change). Colours must be `#rrggbb`, the rest one of a few words, so no club text reaches the page as style. Removing a scheduled event removes its design. `PackArt` takes a `design` prop and still draws at 220x330 scaled; never size design text from the width. Admin: the Pack designs tab (`pages/admin/PackDesigns.jsx`) saves style as you go (debounced, only what changed) and uploads pictures on choosing. No player-keyed column, so nothing for `merge_carry`.
