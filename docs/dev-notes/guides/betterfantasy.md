# BetterFantasyCricket (club fantasy competition)

Code: `backend/app/services/fantasy_engine.py` (rounds, pool, settlement), `fantasy_squad.py`, `fantasy_draft.py`, `routers/fantasy.py` (club admin), `routers/public_fantasy.py` (member play), `frontend/src/pages/admin/fantasy`, `frontend/src/pages/fantasy`. Checks: `backend/verification/verify_fantasy_rounds.py`, `verify_fantasy_unsettle.py`.

- A round's `status` is `upcoming | scored | unsettled`. `unsettled` means an admin took an ended round back out of settlement. Every public read treats it as not scored; the nightly job and Settle due rounds skip it (`fantasy_engine.AUTO_SETTLE_SKIP`). Use that tuple, never a bare `!= "scored"`, for anything that settles unattended.
- `settle_round` writes player scores, pool totals, squad round rows, season totals, head-to-head results, the free-transfer rollover and the status. `unsettle_round` must undo each. Add a line there when settlement starts writing something new.
- A squad's round row also holds what the manager did before lock (`chip_used`, `transfers_made`, `transfer_hit`). Unsettle keeps any row with those and only clears scoring. Never delete such a row.
- `refresh_live_round` writes provisional player, squad and ladder rows for a running round, so a long round shows points. It must never grant the rollover, fill head-to-head results or mark the round scored. Anything shown as "live" that adds a round's points to `squad.total_points` has to swap out the stored provisional figure first, or it counts twice.
- The free-transfer rollover is not idempotent: re-settling an already scored round banks another one. Unsettle takes one back (floored at 0, squads holding a wildcard or free hit left alone).
- Running scores: `services/fantasy_live.live_pass` (scheduler job `fantasy_live_sync` every 10 minutes; admin `POST /season/{id}/sync-scores`, labelled Sync scores) reads the games CA has not finished through `sync_grassroots_game_level_data(live=True)` and calls `refresh_live_round` only where `round_drift` finds a difference. It never grants the rollover, fills head-to-head, marks a round scored or moves prices: only Settle (Confirm final scores in the UI) does. A round settled on its last morning and still being played is settled again from its stored lineup. Bounds: a season with a round OPEN today (`fantasy_live.round_states`: from its first day to the day before the next round starts, or `FINAL_ROUND_GRACE_DAYS` after the last round; never a round whose window has not begun or one an admin unsettled), club-local 07:00 to 22:00 (a round whose own window is over is looked at only at `ENDED_ROUND_HOURS`), at most `sync.LIVE_MAX_GAMES` (40) per pass, one game at a time, skipped while the club has its own sync in flight. Off per season with `rules.live_sync` false (`PATCH /season/{id}/rules`, absent means on) or everywhere with `FANTASY_LIVE_SYNC_ENABLED=false`. `fantasy_rounds.live_checked_at` (migration 361) is display only. `verify_fantasy_live_sync.py` (paired with `frontend/verification/verify_fantasy_live_sync_browser.mjs`).
- Known quirk, left alone on purpose: `settle_all_fantasy` and Settle due rounds settle a round when `end_date <= today`, which is the morning of its last day, before that day's games. The live pass and `refresh_recent_rounds` keep its points right, but the round reads scored early, and moving the threshold would change when the free transfer banks and when next-round transfers open. Decide that on its own.
- Rounds vs the draw: `generate_rounds` never rewrites a `scored` round and numbers rounds by position in the draw, so a round scored before the draw was complete (the second week of a two day round, a later fixture) stays on short dates and every later round is numbered from the wrong place; a game dated outside every round's window scores for nobody and no sync helps. `fantasy_engine.draw_windows` is the one read of the draw. `services/fantasy_realign` (`GET`/`POST /season/{id}/rounds/realign`, banner and Fix round dates on the overview) plans then applies it, all or nothing: a `widen` (draw window holds the stored one) moves the dates and settles again from the stored lineups; a `replace` goes through `unsettle_round`, takes the draw's dates, first kick-off and default lock, and is left `upcoming`. A `replace` is refused while a chip, transfer, hit, points, queued transfer, head-to-head result, transaction or hand-typed score hangs off it. Teams created after the round was settled get their free transfers put back after the unsettle, and a team stamped `joined_round` = N+1 (it joined while N+1 was the open round) goes back to N when round N is replaced, or it is skipped for N and shows 0 (`repair_joined_rounds`, run at the end of apply and on every live pass, read from the audit entries, only teams created before them and only while N is unscored). My Team shows the running round from `fanLive` while the round is locked and being played, the last scored round otherwise. One `fantasy_realign_rounds` audit entry holds before and after (undo is by hand: set the dates back and settle). `verify_fantasy_realign.py`.
- Other managers' teams: `GET /public/fantasy/{token}/team/{squad_id}` (`public_fantasy.team_view`, opened from ladder and league rows when `teams_visible`) returns ONLY the team locked for the most recently LOCKED round (`_last_locked_round`: latest `lock_at` in the past, scored or not), read from that squad's `fantasy_squad_round_scores.lineup`. Never read the squad's picks for a scored round: `fantasy_queue.apply_pending` rewrites them at settlement, so between settle and the next lock they hold the NEXT round's team. Picks are read only for a locked, unscored round with no row yet (frozen then). Never add prices, bank, free transfers, queued transfers or the next round's chips to that response (`verify_fantasy_team_view.py` pins the field list and the post-settle case). Needs a signed-in manager with a squad in the same league; a team whose `joined_round` is after the locked round has none.
- Ladder while a round is live: `_league_standings` uses the live round (`_live_round`: first unscored round, once locked) as the GW column and takes ITS points off the total for the arrows; otherwise the last scored round as before. The ladder response carries `live` and `teams_visible`; the screen reloads every minute while live.
- Super admin entry: Better HQ > Clubs & Data > Fantasy Competitions (`/admin/super/fantasy`). Open uses `switchClub(clubId, 'admin/fantasy')`.
- Admin "view as a team": `POST /club-admin/fantasy/view-as` sets a separate 3 hour cookie (`bs_fantasy_viewas`, a JWT with `typ: fantasy_viewas`) that `public_fantasy._read_session` honours ahead of the member's own cookie. It is read only through ONE router dependency, `_block_writes_while_viewing`; any new public write is covered automatically, so never add a write route outside that router. Only paths containing `/view-as/` (switch, exit) pass the guard. Never mint a member session for the admin: it would overwrite a real manager's sign-in.
- Every table with a player FK is `ON DELETE CASCADE` or `SET NULL`, so each must be on `merge_carry.CARRIED` (the Fantasy ones are, with club guards). A new fantasy table that points at a player goes on that list in the same change. `fantasy_merge_repair` restores picks lost before that fix from the stored lineup snapshots, which is why unsettling (which clears a kept row's lineup) is also the end of that evidence.
- A hand-typed round score is a `fantasy_player_round_scores` row with `breakdown.manual` set. Settle, the live refresh and unsettle must never overwrite or delete it; only `clear_manual_score` does. Admin pick edits (`add_squad_player`) warn on size and quota, never refuse, and re-score from a chosen round through `fantasy_engine.rescore_from_round`.
- `settle_round` banks the free transfer only when the round was not already scored (`first_settle`), so re-settling is safe.
- Deleted Fantasy picks can be read back from the live table until VACUUM clears them: `fantasy_merge_repair.read_deleted_picks` (pageinspect, superuser, read only) behind `restore_fantasy_merged_picks --from-dead-rows`. Do it soon after the loss and never run VACUUM FULL first. Backups are age-encrypted and the key is not kept on the server, so a backup route needs someone who holds the key.
- Hand-added pool players ("Add new player") have no games; their twin with games is found by `fantasy_pool_check` (sorted-words name key, so "Taylor, Ashton" matches "Ashton Taylor") and merged from `HandAddedPlayersCard` via `POST /season/{id}/merge-player`. That route accepts only a pair the list shows, needs MANAGE_MERGES as well as MANAGE_FANTASY, runs the normal `_merge_players_core` and then re-scores from round 1. `verify_fantasy_player_merge.py` section 13 seeds a player into every Fantasy table that has a player FK (read from `pg_constraint`) and fails if a new one is not on `merge_carry.CARRIED`.
- Games are the club's by `club_game_sql` (own fixture or one of the two sides), never by `seasons.organisation_id`; included grade ids are widened by name key through `club_grade_rows` (`_grade_scope`), and the four per-game reads join `players` on the club so a shared fixture's other side is not scored. `verify_fantasy_shared_fixture.py`.
- A scored round is checked against its scorecards (`round_drift`) and settled again by `refresh_recent_rounds` (nightly job, Settle due rounds) for 14 days. Re-settling a scored round scores each squad from its STORED lineup (`from_snapshot`), never today's picks, because managers transfer between rounds; only an admin edit of the picks themselves passes `live_picks=True`. A change to scoring settings therefore also reaches rounds scored in the last fortnight. `verify_fantasy_late_scorecards.py`.
- "Added by hand" in the Fantasy admin means a player record with no `grassroots_id` and no `playhq_id` (created by Add new player). `fantasy_pool_players.role_source = 'admin'` only means an admin set the role, so it must not be used for this.
- To find out why a player who played is on 0, run `python -m app.scripts.fantasy_trace_player <org> "<name or id>"` (`fantasy_pool_check.trace_player`): it lists every record with that name, their pool entry, merges, each game they have rows in and the first scoring filter it fails. Two records with the same display name are normal at a club after sync (the aggregate feed and the scorecards each make one); the trace shows which one the pool holds. It reads the effective views, so imported (`manual`) games and a synced game hidden by a preferred paired import are shown too.
- Grades switched off for a season are `rules.excluded_grade_keys` (name keys, not ids), read by `fantasy_engine._grade_scope`; `_grade_clause` treats an empty list as "nothing counts", only None as "all".
- Choosing grades: `GET/PUT /club-admin/fantasy/season/{id}/grades` (`fantasy_grades.grade_options`, `GradeScopeCard` on the Scoring page). It stores switched-OFF grade name keys in `rules.excluded_grade_keys`, refuses switching everything off or an unknown key, keeps a switch-off for a grade with no row yet, supersedes the older `included_grade_ids`, and re-scores from round 1 (lineups kept). `settle_round` now deletes player-round rows the data no longer gives and `_refresh_pool_totals` zeroes a player left with none, otherwise a switched-off grade would leave stale points. `verify_fantasy_grade_scope.py`.
- A player who played and has no rows at all for the game (trace shows no game, scorecard shows them) was dropped by sync because their id on the team sheet is not one we stored. Sync now attaches by full name (`services/participant_names`); sync also repairs an already stored game from the scorecard it holds (`participant_relink.repair_stored_game`); `python -m app.scripts.relink_rostered_players <org> [--apply]` remains for a club that wants a dry run first. A hand-added pool player is merged into their synced twin automatically when unambiguous (`services/hand_added_merge`, undoable from the merge log); `HandAddedPlayersCard` is for the cases it leaves. Never delete and re-pull a synced game to fix this: `fee_match_days` cascades and an imported pair is unlinked.
- Prices are DERIVED, never added to: `fantasy_pricing.recompute_prices` replays every `scored` round onto `base_price` (`peer_moves`: each player who played is measured against the other players in THAT round for their price, never against what the starting price implies; move = surprise / `MOVE_SCALE` 200 in tenths, capped at `MAX_MOVE` 0.5, floor `PRICE_MIN`, nobody moves in a round of fewer than `MIN_PEERS` players, only players who played move). A rule that scores against an absolute expectation inflates every cheap player, so keep any change zero-sum across the round and check it with a cheap player on 41, 82 and 150 points and writes `current_price` and `price_change` (the latest scored round's move). `settle_round`, `unsettle_round`, `build_pool(reset)` and the nightly job all call it. Anything that changes what a round scored must go through those, never `UPDATE fantasy_pool_players SET current_price`. `base_price` is only overwritten on a reset rebuild. A hand-typed price sets `price_locked` and is skipped; migration 326 locked every price that differed from its base once, when the column was created. `verify_fantasy_pricing.py`.
- A sale returns the player's CURRENT price (`public_fantasy._sell_value`, mirrored by `sellValue` in `Transfers.jsx`), so a rise banks and a fall costs. `purchase_price` is only the price paid. Any new place that shows a price shows `PriceArrow` (`pages/fantasy/ui.jsx`): green ▲ up, red ▼ down, nothing when flat.
- After `lock_at` a round freezes XI, captain, chips and squad rebuilds (`_round_locked`). A transfer then goes to `fantasy_queued_transfers` (`fantasy_queue.queue_transfer`) and the squad's picks are NOT touched: `settle_round` scores the live picks, then `fantasy_queue.apply_pending` carries the queue out at the post-settle prices (after `recompute_prices`) and tallies it on the next round. `unsettle_round` calls `revert_applied` first, or the round would be re-scored with the post-transfer XI. Never write a squad's picks for a locked round from anywhere else. A queued transfer that can't happen is `cancelled` with a reason, never dropped, and no queue row is deleted by code. Both player columns are on `merge_carry.CARRIED`; `verify_fantasy_lock_queue.py`.
- `lock_at` comes from `fantasy_engine.default_lock_at`: `LOCK_LEAD_MINUTES` (60) before the round's first match, read in the CLUB's timezone (season rule `lock_timezone`, else the state's zone from `organisations.state` or its linked `marketing_clubs` row, else Melbourne), unless `fantasy_rounds.lock_manual` is set; the upsert in `generate_rounds` keeps a manual time. The kick-off is the earliest `matchSchedule.startDateTime` on the round's first day from the live calendar, read as club-local wall clock like the Fixtures page does (a midnight time is a date with no time), and is kept on `fantasy_rounds.first_start`; with none the lock is 09:00 local (`lock_time` rule). A refresh that gets no time for the same first day keeps the stored one. Games hold dates only, so never derive a lock from `played_at`. Edit with `PATCH /club-admin/fantasy/rounds/{id}/lock` (refused for a scored round). `python -m app.scripts.fantasy_refresh_lock_times <org|all> [--apply]` rewrites the defaults now; `verify_fantasy_lock_first_match.py`.
- A manager who joins after the season is `active` starts level with the lowest team: `fantasy_squads.catchup_points` (fixed at join, from SCORED rounds only) is added back by `recompute_squad_totals`, so never set `total_points` on its own; `joined_round` stops any re-score from crediting rounds before they joined. The round lock still blocks joining, and rebuilding ends once their first round locks (`_may_rebuild`). `verify_fantasy_late_join.py`.
- Price movers are read from `fantasy_pool_players.price_change` (the latest scored round's move) in the member lists (`MoverChips`/`byMover` in `pages/fantasy/ui.jsx`) and from `GET /club-admin/fantasy/season/{id}/price-movements` (history replayed through `fantasy_pricing.season_history`, same maths as the nightly recompute) on the admin Price movements page. A new player list shows the chips.
- `fielding_stats` has one row per INNINGS fielded (a two-day game gives two): fielding figures are summed across a game's rows, never assigned (`_round_player_scores`; `verify_fantasy_multi_innings_fielding.py`).
- A player on far fewer points than their scorecard on PlayCricket gives (a 40 odd on 1, four wickets on 24) may be a game stored while CA still had it in play: Fantasy scores the stored per-innings rows, which sync did not update once stored. Sync now refreshes a recent game from its COMPLETED card (`services/card_figures_refresh.py`, data-sources guide rule 56) and the nightly drift check then settles the round again. For a game already stored short: `python -m app.scripts.refresh_card_figures <org>` (dry run lists every figure), then `fantasy_round_drift <org> --apply`. `verify_live_card_refresh.py`.
- A saved squad pick keeps the role and price it was bought at (`fantasy_squad_players.role`, `purchase_price`), but `POST /squad` validates against the pool's CURRENT role and price. Any screen that counts roles or spend for a squad about to be saved (`Pick.jsx`) must read them from the pool, never from the saved row. Do not sync the stored role to the pool: a live squad has to stay valid under the quota it was built with.
- All teams at once: `GET /public/fantasy/{token}/teams` (`public_fantasy.teams_view`, the Teams screen's List and Cards views) returns every team in the viewer's league through the SAME `_locked_team` helper `team_view` uses, so the locked-round rule and the field list cannot drift between the two; it adds only `rank` and `rank_delta`. `_enrich_lineup(info=...)` takes a preloaded `_player_info` and still looks up any player it lacks (a live-scored lineup has no stored row), so a bulk call never shows "?" names. `verify_fantasy_team_view.py` section 8 pins it, `frontend/verification/verify_fantasy_teams_browser.mjs` drives the screen.
- The card stack (`components/CardSwap.jsx`) is gsap, driven by next, prev and goTo with no timer; only `MAX_DEPTH` cards behind the front are drawn, so a club of fifty teams does not run off the screen. It sets `user-select: none` on purpose: a swipe over selected text starts a native drag and the browser cancels the gesture. A shared card is a second, flat `TeamCard` rendered off screen (`lib/exportNodePng.js`), never the 3D one.
- My Team has a Sheet / Cards switch (`pages/fantasy/PlayerCards.jsx`, remembered in `localStorage` `bfc_team_view`); both views share `openId` and the `actions(p)` buttons, so the captain rules (open round only, never the same player as captain and vice) are enforced in one place. `cards` must be defined AFTER `actions` in `Team.jsx`: it reads it at render and a `const` read early is a blank-screen crash (`verify_fantasy_my_team_cards_browser.mjs` caught it).
- Every exported card goes through `lib/exportNodePng.js`. modern-screenshot adds `font.cssText` to the image as it is and never fetches the files in it, and an image loads nothing from the network, so the exporter fetches the Google Fonts CSS, keeps the latin faces of the families the node uses, and inlines each file as a data URL. Without it, exports used a fallback font while the box sizes (frozen from the live page) stayed those of the real font, which clipped text. Do not copy the old inline exporter into a new card.
