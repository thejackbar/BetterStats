# Brag Plan: BetterSocials, quick template switching

## Angle
Pull the data once per post type, then jump between layouts to show how quickly the design changes. Each post type uses a different real club (crests, sponsors and adult player photos from their public BetterCricket pages). Same style as the other videos: BetterSocials pink aurora, music only, camera zooms, BetterCricket logo bug.

## Scenes (about 3.7 minutes)
Intro, start screen, Lineup (Applecross: T11 transparent cut-out photo with milestone watch, debut, captain and sponsor, then T12 with sponsors from Stats, then T4, T9, T2), Fixtures (Scarborough: crests, add a page, FX3, FX2, FX5, FX6), Results (Bannockburn: RR7, RR2, RR3, RR6), Final Score (Hilton Bicton: RS3, RS5, RS7 with a background photo), Milestones (Kalamunda: MS1, MS2), Team of the Week (High Wycombe: TW1, TW2), Events (Gisborne), Announcement C5 (Applecross), drag and drop customisation (Gisborne), template wall, close, end card.

## Data and accuracy
Results are derived from the scores: every margin, winner, batting total, bowler's wickets and overs add up (checked by hand against the posters). Milestone flags use real career totals; the Applecross flag says "3 games from 250" because the player is on 247. Opposition crests and opposition players are real clubs and players but the fixtures and scores are demo data. Adult players only; no Jack Barendse.
